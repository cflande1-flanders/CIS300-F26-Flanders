Is Nesta Being Called Back to the Dusk Court?

Nesta Archeron has been connected to some of Prythian's oldest and most powerful magic since she emerged from the Cauldron.

She took power directly from it, wielded the Dread Trove, and retained some of that power even after giving most of it back. More recently, Nesta became directly involved in uncovering the lost history of the Dusk Court and proved capable of wielding or interacting with Dusk-connected weapons and Made objects, including Gwydion and Truth-Teller.

That raises a question:

What if Nesta's remaining power is connected to Dusk?

MoreLess

Nesta's silver flames have largely been associated with death, but we still do not fully understand what her remaining power is or why the Mother allowed her to keep it.

The Dusk Court, meanwhile, was erased from Prythian's known history. Its people disappeared, its land became the Prison, and much of its true history remained hidden until the events of House of Flame and Shadow.

Nesta now sits at the intersection of both mysteries.

The Northern Horizon

On October 22, 2026, Sarah J. Maas released a Splintered Harmony teaser centered on Nesta. In it, Nesta feels something calling to her from beyond the northern horizon. She questions whether the call is a “siren song,” luring her away from what mattered and who mattered.

The direction is difficult to ignore.

With the Prison and the lost Dusk Court already positioned within Nesta's story, the reference to the northern horizon gives the existing theory a new geographical clue. More importantly, something is not simply waiting for Nesta to find it. Something appears to be calling her toward it.

The previous day's Cassian teaser may make this even more significant. Cassian lets Nesta walk away from him and out the front door, and Nesta does not look back. Paired with Nesta's fear that the mysterious call may be luring her away from “who mattered,” the two excerpts could be connected.

Could Nesta Restore Dusk?

If the call is coming from the former Dusk Court, why is it calling to her?

The language surrounding The Valkyrie Cycle repeatedly emphasizes things that have been broken, lost, remade, and reclaimed. Dusk is one of the greatest examples of something lost in Prythian's history.

Nesta could be part of bringing it back.

That does not necessarily mean becoming High Lady of a restored Dusk Court. Her role could instead involve awakening the land, restoring its magic, freeing what was suppressed there, returning its history to Prythian, or creating a new home for the reborn Valkyries.

And if Dusk itself is calling to her, Splintered Harmony may be where that restoration begins.

What to Watch For

Watch for Nesta's magic reacting to the land or the Prison, further references to the north, silver flames appearing around Dusk-linked magic, new information about the power the Mother left with Nesta, and any explanation for why she can wield the Trove, Gwydion, and Truth-Teller.
