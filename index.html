A Court of Forgotten Melody Riddle Trail

# Riddle Clue Our Guess Why the Clue Fits Wagner / Ring Connection Status
1 “A RAINBOW of canals and art / AMSTERDAM will mark the start” Amsterdam Bloomsbury explicitly names Amsterdam. The Amsterdam Wagner Society was founded in 1883, shortly after Wagner's death, and became an important center for Wagner performances in the Netherlands. ✅ Confirmed
2 “Where another WALL came down” Berlin Berlin Wall. Berlin has a substantial Wagner/Ring performance history, including complete Ring cycles. ✅ Confirmed
3 “Winnow o'er seas to where STARS live” Los Angeles / New York Both cities are associated with entertainment stars, although Los Angeles is the stronger literal clue because of Hollywood. Los Angeles: LA Opera presented a complete Ring in 2010 and organized Ring Festival LA. New York: the Metropolitan Opera presented the first complete Ring Cycle in the Western Hemisphere in 1889. ⏳
4 “Then to a city that's built on GOLD / Emerge from its CAVES for a secret told” Johannesburg Johannesburg grew from the Witwatersrand gold rush; the Sterkfontein Caves are near Johannesburg. Thomas Quinlan's Opera Company staged a Wagner Centenary Festival in Johannesburg in 1913, including Die Walküre, along with Tristan und Isolde, Lohengrin, Tannhäuser, and Die Meistersinger. ⏳
5 “On to a seat of ROYALTY / A CLOCK tolls in the vicinity” London British monarchy + Big Ben. Wagner visited London several times. In 1855 he conducted Philharmonic Society concerts there, and in 1877 he conducted concerts at Royal Albert Hall connected with financing Bayreuth. ⏳
6 “Down and UNDER to the southernmost coast” Melbourne Australia = “Down Under”; Melbourne strongly fits the southern-coast clue. Thomas Quinlan's company toured Australia after South Africa. In 1913, Melbourne hosted Australia's first complete Ring Cycle, and the company performed two complete Ring cycles during its Melbourne season. ⏳
7 “For the last we'll WELCOME YOU BACK / To where we began this new soundtrack” Amsterdam Returns to the explicitly stated starting city. Returning to Amsterdam closes the geographical route into a literal cycle, alongside the Valkyrie Cycle branding and the campaign's musical terminology. ⏳
